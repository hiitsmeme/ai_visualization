Using AI helped us understand the project in a broad way, without going too deep into code syntax. We found the process to be similar to delegating tasks to a team. Instead of focusing on how to implement every detail ourselves, we could describe what we wanted to achieve and let AI suggest possible approaches, implementations, and visualizations.

Throughout the project, we noticed that communicating with LLMs also influenced the way we formulated our prompts. Over time, our writing became more structured, detailed, and similar to AI-generated text, which generally led to better and more accurate outputs. We learned that giving clear instructions, providing context, and specifying the expected results made a significant difference.
AI was useful for exploratory data analysis and debugging. It also helped us turn observations into new hypotheses and decide which experiments to perform next. 

However, we also realized that AI-generated solutions could not always be trusted without verification. Sometimes the code needed adjustments, and the interpretations of the results had to be critically evaluated. In particular, when brainstorming with AI, we noticed that it tends to get trapped by literal wording rather than understanding the core semantics.

While AI significantly accelerated the technical parts of the project, understanding the results and deciding what was worth investigating remained our responsibility. 

The experience showed us that effectively working with AI is a skill in itself.
